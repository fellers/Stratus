# Decision

- Use Gemfan Hurricane 3520 three-blade propellers as the standard propeller for Stratus Rev A.

# Status

- Accepted

# Context

The propeller is responsible for converting motor torque into thrust. Selection must provide stable flight characteristics while remaining affordable and readily replaceable during development.

## Selection Criteria

- Efficiency
- Durability
- Cost
- Availability
- Compatibility with selected motors
- Flight stability

# Decision Criteria

- Designed for 3.5-inch platforms
- Compatible with selected EX1404 motors
- Excellent community reputation
- Low replacement cost
- Smooth flight characteristics

# Alternatives Considered

## Two-Blade Propellers

### Pros

- Higher efficiency
- Longer flight times

### Cons

- Lower control authority
- Reduced stability during aggressive maneuvering

## Generic 3.5-inch Propellers

### Pros

- Lower cost

### Cons

- Less consistent manufacturing quality
- Less predictable flight characteristics

# Consequences

## Advantages

- Stable flight behavior
- Excellent compatibility with selected motors
- Low replacement cost during testing

## Disadvantages

- Slightly reduced efficiency compared to two-blade designs

# Future Considerations

Future revisions should evaluate alternate propeller geometries as aircraft weight and mission profiles evolve.
