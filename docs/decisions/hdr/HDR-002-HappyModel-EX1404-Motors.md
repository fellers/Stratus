# Decision

- Use the HappyModel EX1404 3500KV brushless motors as the primary propulsion motors for Stratus Rev A.

# Status

- Accepted

# Context

The propulsion system is responsible for generating the thrust required for stable flight while supporting future autonomous control algorithms. Motor selection must balance efficiency, cost, weight, performance, and compatibility with the selected 3.5-inch platform.

## Selection Criteria

- Cost
- Performance
- Community adoption
- Reliability
- Compatibility with 3.5-inch propellers
- Future scalability

# Decision Criteria

- Well-established reputation within the FPV community
- Compatible with 3.5-inch propellers
- Suitable performance for 4S operation
- Excellent value relative to competing motors
- Widely available replacement parts
- Provides sufficient thrust for future payload expansion

# Alternatives Considered

## EMAX ECO Series

### Pros

- Excellent build quality
- Strong community reputation

### Cons

- Higher cost
- Reduced value for educational objectives

## Flash Hobby Motors

### Pros

- Low cost
- Readily available

### Cons

- Less established long-term reliability
- More inconsistent quality control

## Larger 1804/2004 Motors

### Pros

- Higher thrust
- Better future payload capacity

### Cons

- Increased weight
- Reduced efficiency on a 3.5-inch platform
- Less suitable for Rev A objectives

# Consequences

## Advantages

- Excellent cost-to-performance ratio
- Lightweight propulsion system
- Supports modular platform objectives
- Proven compatibility with selected propellers

## Disadvantages

- Limited payload capability compared to larger motors
- Future 5-inch platform will likely require larger motors

# Future Considerations

Future Stratus revisions may transition to larger motors for 5-inch or larger aircraft while maintaining the existing software architecture and propulsion interfaces.
